About

I'm Jay Friesen, a California-based artist. Throughout my life, I have immersed myself in various artistic mediums, including film, writing, and music. Art is my way to share a piece of myself with the world.

My creative journey is defined by storytelling. Every project is an opportunity to share something with the world, from a complex theme to a small idea. I take pride in crafting stories that reflect my experiences and insights, whether through a captivating film, an evocative piece of writing, or an original song.

This site serves as my portfolio and blog, showcasing both my work and my thoughts on film and music. Even this site itself is my own work; I coded it line by line. I also have a Projects tab where you can check out some of my other work from my YouTube channel to some cool web projects.

Thank you for taking the time to explore my site. I hope you find something that resonates with you.
